# XYZ Learning

**XYZ Learning Private Limited** is an education company based in Kolkata, India. We build tools that help Indian students learn, and help teachers and education businesses earn from their work.

## XYZ Notes

[XYZ Notes](https://xyznotes.in) is our notes marketplace: published chapter PDF notes for Indian students and exams, with prices in rupees. Browse what’s live, preview before buying, and teachers can sell notes they have already written.

- Website: [xyznotes.in](https://xyznotes.in)
- Browse notes: [xyznotes.in/browse](https://xyznotes.in/browse)
- Marketplace: [xyznotes.in/marketplace](https://xyznotes.in/marketplace)

## XYZ Education and XYZ One

[XYZ Education](https://xyzeducation.in) is our marketplace for classes, teachers, and classrooms.

[XYZ One](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.xyzeducation.app) is the Android app for studying with AI, finding tutors, teaching classes, and earning from your skills. It is available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.xyzeducation.app).
